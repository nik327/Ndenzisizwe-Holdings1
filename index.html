Company profile

Built for reliable operations, from the plant floor to the supply chain.

Ndenzisizwe Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a South African company committed to delivering reliable maintenance, engineering support, construction, and project management services. We provide skilled personnel, quality workmanship, and safety-focused solutions to meet our clients' operational requirements.

Our work spans four core areas — mining & plant maintenance, industrial & MRO supplies, production & packaging materials, and supply chain & logistics — backed by a wider bench of facilities, training, professional and IT support services.

Primary divisions

Four core divisions cover the day-to-day of keeping a plant, quarry or supply chain running.

Mining & Plant Maintenance

Skilled mechanical, electrical and civil personnel for plant upkeep and mine-site support — from routine maintenance to full breakdown response.

  • General plant maintenance

  • Conveyor belt splicing
  • Material-handling maintenance
  • Liners, bolts & mill liners
  • Idlers, rollers & pulleys
  • Steel & pipes
  • Drilling & blasting
  • Mining operational support
  • Mining equipment & MRO supplies
